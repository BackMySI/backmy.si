Privacy

Short and plain. Last updated October 2026.

What we keep

Only things you've already made public: your handle, links to your work and tip jar, a line about what you built, and whether you've said yes to being featured. We never ask for your email, address or payment details.

Why

To ask if you'd like a card on the board and an X post, and, if you say yes, to show them. We rely on your yes for anything we publish, and on a legitimate interest for keeping the short note while we wait for your reply.

Checking first

Before we feature anyone, we look over their public posts to check they're a good fit. We don't keep reasons, and nothing about the check is ever published. If we decide not to feature someone, we keep only their handle and the date, for 90 days, then delete it.

How long

  • If you don't reply, we delete our note about you after 3 months.
  • If you say no, we delete it straight away and won't ask again.
  • If you're featured, your card stays until you ask us to take it down.

Your ideas stay yours

We only show what you've already made public yourself. We never ask for, keep or post anything unreleased or secret, so please don't send it. We don't claim any rights in your work or ideas. A feature isn't a patent or any kind of legal protection, but it is a dated public record of what you'd shared.

Removal

Ask to be removed any time and it's done the same day. Message @backmysi on X and we'll remove your card, page and share picture the same day. X may keep showing an old preview picture for a few days after that, which we can't control. Our other accounts are only holding the name and aren't monitored, so X is the one place to ask.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you at any time, through the same message. If you're unhappy with how we've handled it, you can complain to the ICO.

On this site

No cookies, no tracking, no ads. Fonts are loaded from this site, not from anyone else.
